EmojiRL is a casual roguelike where emojis are the tileset and the core gameplay mechanic.

Your emoji collection IS your soul. Collect, equip, and strategically store emojis that grant powers, passives, and stats as you descend through procedurally generated floors.

CLASSES
* Wizard — Ranged autobattler with powerful area abilities
* Ninja — High mobility and stealth mechanics
* Ranger — Ranged specialist with ammo and survival mechanics
* Cowboy — Unarmed brawler who dual-wields guns when found

FEATURES
* Emoji as core identity — emojis function as equipment, passive bonuses, and active abilities
* 4 equipment slots — Body, Main Hand, Off Hand, and Accessory
* Line of sight with raycasted fog of war and memory of explored areas
* Mood system — your emotional state affects gameplay
* Enemy personalities — cowardly, berserker, packHunter, and silent enemies
* Autoexplore — let the game explore for you
* Procedurally generated floors
* Fully offline — no internet required, no accounts, no ads
* All progress saved locally on your device

PRIVACY
EmojiRL collects no data whatsoever. All game state is stored locally on your device.
